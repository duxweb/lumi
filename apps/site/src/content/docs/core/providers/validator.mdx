---
title: "Validator"
description: "Validator 接口的方法、参数和返回值。"
---

Business validation of a form's values. `rules` is whatever the page gives
(`rules` of `submitForm` or `useForm`); the validator decides how to read
them, e.g. as a JSON Schema. Without one configured, rules are refused.

```typescript
interface Validator
```

| 名称 | 类型 | 必填 | 说明 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `validate` | `(rules: ValidationRules, values: Record<string, unknown>) => ValidationIssue[] \| Promise<ValidationIssue[]>` | 是 | The issues of `values` under `rules`, each at a dotted field path; none when they pass. May be asynchronous. |
